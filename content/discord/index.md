## <span style="color:var(--darkBlue)">Join the Discord Server</span>

The Timbre 2026 Discord server is designed to make the conference experience as connected and accessible as possible. Discord is a free platform available on any device: you can download the app or use it directly from your browser at [discord.com](https://discord.com).

The server has text channels organized around conference themes and sessions, so you can jump into the conversations that matter most to you.

## <span style="color:var(--darkBlue)">What You Can Do on the Server</span>

- **Connect around research themes**: Each channel is organized by topic so you can find and introduce yourself to attendees who share your area of interest, and and also discover other research themes.

- **Engage with speakers**: Post questions or comments related to a talk and speakers can respond asynchronously before or after their session.

- **Stay updated**: Get real-time announcements from the organizers, including any schedule changes or logistical updates.

- **Sort out logistics**: Use the dedicated lodging and travel channels to coordinate with fellow attendees on accommodation, rides, or local tips.

- **Network beyond the conference**: The server stays active after the event, so conversations and connections don't have to stop when the conference does.
